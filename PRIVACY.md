# Privacy policy – LPT Pro

*Last updated: November 2026*

LPT Pro copies photos and videos from an iPhone connected by USB cable to a folder
you choose on your computer.

**What it collects: nothing.**

- LPT Pro has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- It does not send your photos, videos, file names or any other information over the
  internet. It talks only to the iPhone, through the USB service on your own computer.
- It reads from the iPhone and never changes or deletes anything on it.

**What it keeps on your computer**

- The photos and videos you copy, in the backup folder you choose.
- A small hidden folder `.lptpro` inside that backup folder. It records which photos were
  saved as JPEG, so they are recognised as backed up.
- Settings, a fingerprint cache that makes the backup check faster, and a log file
  (`lptpro.log`), in the app's own data folder. The log contains file names and
  technical messages, never the photos themselves. It stays on your computer unless you
  choose to send it to someone.
- Temporary previews while the iPhone is connected. These are deleted when you
  disconnect, unplug or close the app.

**Links**

A few buttons open web pages in your browser, for example Apple's page about its USB
driver. Only you decide when to press them.

**Questions**

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
